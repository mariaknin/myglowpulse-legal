Privacy Policy

MyGlowPulse app (iOS and Android) · Last updated: October 17, 2026

Draft — this text still has to be legally reviewed before final publication. The French version prevails.

MyGlowPulse is a skincare routine tracking app. Every day you take a selfie (the "Pulse"); an image analysis service computes five skin criteria from the photo, and the app helps you follow how they evolve along with your routine. This policy explains what data we collect, why, where it is processed, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller is Marie Aknin, publisher of MyGlowPulse. For any question or to exercise your rights: maknin@myglowpulse.ai.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for targeted advertising and we do not track you across apps.

3. Where your data is processed (processors)

  • Supabase — database, authentication and photo storage. Hosting region: [to be confirmed in the Supabase dashboard].
  • Modal (United States) — hosts our image analysis service. Your photo is sent to it during the Pulse to compute the scores; it is not kept there after the analysis.
  • Anthropic and OpenRouter (United States) — artificial intelligence providers used, through our own intermediary server, to import your routine by scanning your products (recognising a product from its photo or label). They only receive the product photo and the text needed for recognition: never your selfie, email or phone number.
  • Sentry — crash reports.
  • Expo (United States) — push notification delivery.

When data is transferred outside the European Union, these transfers are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent mechanism offered by the provider.

4. Your consent for photos and AI

Before your first Pulse, the app explicitly asks your permission to send your selfie to our analysis service and to use third-party AI services. Until you agree, no photo leaves your phone. You can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to us or by deleting your account.

5. Retention

  • Account data, photos, scores, routine, badges and reminder events: for as long as your account exists. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Photo sent to the analysis service (Modal): only for the duration of the analysis.
  • Notification token: deleted when you log out or delete your account.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): per Sentry's retention period (90 days at most).

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data (profile, photos, scores, routine) directly in the app: Account tab → Delete my account. You can also write to us at maknin@myglowpulse.ai.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise them, write to us. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or your local authority.

8. Minors

If you are under 15, you need the agreement of a person with parental authority to use MyGlowPulse.

9. Security

Exchanges with our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your data is restricted to your account by database-level security rules, and your photos are stored in a private space, only reachable through temporary links.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If there is a significant change, we will let you know in the app.
